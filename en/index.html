Estante

A notes app for iPhone, with your notebooks on a bookcase.

Write, record audio and photograph the board. Each notebook sits on a shelf, and everything you keep stays on your iPhone. No account and no ads.

Coming soon to the App Store

How the bookcase works

The bookcase holds your notebooks. Each shelf is for one subject, like College, Work or Home. On each shelf, you put a notebook for each topic.

  • Tap a notebook and it comes off the shelf, open to the contents, with the list of pages.
  • Double-tap to open right to the page where you stopped. The red ribbon marks the spot.
  • Choose the cover from six styles, with the icon and color you want.
  • Lock a notebook, and it only opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode.
  • What you delete stays in the Trash for 30 days, at the bottom of the bookcase. During that time, you can bring it back.

How to write

The first line becomes the page's name. Then just write. Everything saves on its own, no need to press anything.

  • Headings
  • Lists
  • Tasks
  • Bold and italic
  • Colored pens
  • Highlighters
  • Quotes
  • Callouts
  • Blocks that open and close
  • Formulas
  • Code
  • The paper can be lined, grid, dotted or blank, in eight colors. Each page has its own.
  • The bottom bar shows the tools you choose.
  • Reading mode makes the page read only, without the keyboard.
  • Search finds notebooks, shelves and any word written in your pages.

Photos and audio

The paper clip, at the top of the page, adds photos and audio in the middle of what you write.

  • Record a class, a meeting or an idea. You can lock the screen, and the recording keeps going.
  • Photograph the board and adjust the crop before adding it to the page.
  • Scan a sheet of paper, and it comes out straight, like in a scanner.
  • Select and copy the text in a photo, or add that text to the page.

Widgets and shortcuts

On the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, Estante widgets show a page, a notebook, your tasks, the calendar or where you left off.

  • In Control Center, the New Page and Record Audio buttons. Record Audio opens a page already recording.
  • In the Notes app, tap Share and choose Estante. The note becomes a page in the notebook you choose.
  • Export a notebook as a PDF or as text, to send or print.

Privacy

It stays on your iPhone

Your notebooks, photos and audio are kept on your iPhone.

No account

No sign-up and no login. Just open it and write.

Nothing is collected

We don't see, collect or sell anything you keep.

No ads

The app shows no ads and doesn't track what you do.
