Privacy Policy

What happens to what you keep in Estante.

Updated November 27, 2026.

Estante is a notes app for iPhone, made in Brazil. This policy explains what happens to what you keep in it.

In short

  • Everything you write stays on your iPhone, with a copy in your iCloud.
  • Estante has no server, no account and no login.
  • We don't see, collect or sell anything you keep.
  • The app has no ads and doesn't track what you do.

What stays on your iPhone

The shelves, notebooks, pages, photos and audio you add to the app are kept on your iPhone, in the app's own space. If iCloud Backup is on in iPhone Settings, Apple also keeps a copy along with the rest of the backup, in your iCloud account. We have no access to that backup.

The copy in your iCloud

So your notebooks don't get lost, the app keeps a copy of your shelves, notebooks, pages, photos and audio in your iCloud on its own, from time to time, in the Apple Account on your iPhone. The copy is kept in a part of iCloud only you can access: we don't see anything in it. If you delete the app and download it again, or switch iPhones with the same Apple Account, your notebooks come back on their own.

The copy uses your iCloud storage. If your iCloud is full, turned off for Estante or has no account, the app keeps working on the iPhone only and lets you know once.

The permissions the app asks for

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and it works without the ones you don't give.

  • Camera: to take photos and scan paper into your pages.
  • Microphone: to record audio on your pages.
  • Face ID: to open the notebooks you lock and to confirm what you delete for good. The app never sees your face: the iPhone only says whether it worked.
  • Save to Photos: to save the photos you choose to your Photos.
  • Notifications: for the reminder before your free trial ends, if you want it.

To choose photos, the app uses the iPhone's own photo picker and only receives the ones you choose.

What the iPhone does without anything leaving it

The text in photos is read by the iPhone itself. The free trial reminder is scheduled by the iPhone itself. The widgets and "Save to Estante" read and save in the app's space, on the iPhone itself.

When the app uses the internet

  • Links: when you add a link to a page, the app fetches the site's title and image, like Safari would. When you tap a link, the app may ask the site where the link leads, to open it in the right app. The site receives these requests. The preview stays saved on your iPhone.
  • Purchases: Estante Plus purchases are made through Apple, in the App Store. The app only receives the purchase confirmation. We don't see your name, your email or your payment method.
  • The iCloud copy: the app sends the copy of your notebooks to your iCloud and brings it back when needed. It goes straight to your Apple Account, without passing through us.

Other than that, Estante doesn't send anything to the internet.

How long things are kept

They stay on your iPhone as long as the app is on it. What you delete goes to the app's Trash and stays there for 30 days. After that, it's gone for good. If you delete the app, the iPhone deletes everything that was in it, and the copy in your iCloud stays there, so your notebooks come back when you download the app again. The copy follows your bookcase: what you delete for good leaves it too. To delete the copy, open iPhone Settings, tap your name and iCloud, and look for Estante in the storage. Copies in your iCloud backup follow Apple's rules.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and similar laws in other countries, you can know what is stored, and you can see, correct, take elsewhere and delete your data. In Estante, all of this is already in your hands, inside the app: you see and correct everything in your pages, export your notebooks as PDF and text, and delete whatever you want. Since we don't keep anything, there's nothing of ours to request or delete: the iCloud copy is in your Apple Account, and only you can access it. If you have any questions, write to estante.app@icloud.com.

Children

Estante doesn't collect data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page, with the date at the top.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or the app, write to estante.app@icloud.com.
