Help

The most common questions about Estante. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Getting started

Where do I start?

The first time, the app helps you set up the bookcase, with the shelves and your first notebook. Then the notebook "How to Use Estante", lying on top of the bookcase, explains each part of the app. Feel free to write in it.

To see the welcome again, tap the gear at the top of the bookcase, then "Show the Welcome Again".

How do I create a notebook or a shelf?

Tap the + button at the bottom of the bookcase. There you create a new notebook or a new shelf.

Notebooks

How do I change the cover, lock or export a notebook?

Touch and hold a notebook on the bookcase, then let go. The notebook's options show up, like changing the cover, locking, exporting as a PDF or as text, and deleting.

How do I open a locked notebook?

With Face ID or your iPhone passcode. There's no separate password to remember.

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. What you delete goes to the Trash, the drawer at the bottom of the bookcase. Open the drawer and tap what you want to put back.

Writing

How do I make headings, lists and tasks?

At the start of a line, type one of these signs and a space: # for a heading, ## for a subheading, - for a list, 1. for a numbered list and [ ] for a task. The / shows every type of block for you to choose. The Aa button, on the bottom bar, has all of this too.

How do I change the page's paper?

Tap the paintbrush at the top of the page. There you choose the paper and the color. Each page can have its own paper, and a new page comes like the one before it.

Photos and audio

Audio won't record, or the camera won't open.

The first time, the iPhone asks if Estante can use the microphone and the camera. If you didn't allow it, open iPhone Settings, tap Apps, then Estante, and turn on Microphone or Camera.

Does recording stop when I lock the screen?

No. Recording keeps going, and the time shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a button to stop.

Your notebooks and your iPhone

Where are my notes kept?

On your iPhone. Estante has no server and no account. If iCloud Backup is on, your notes go along in your iPhone's backup. The privacy policy explains everything.

I'm getting a new iPhone. How do I bring my notebooks?

Your notebooks come along when you move everything to the new iPhone, through iCloud Backup or by transferring from one iPhone to the other. For now, Estante doesn't sync with other devices. If you want a separate copy, export your notebooks as a PDF or as text.

Estante Plus

What comes with Estante Plus?

Unlimited notebooks, shelves, and pages, decor for your bookcase and a bookcase style with shelves on the wall. On the free plan, you get 3 notebooks and 3 shelves forever, with up to 5 pages in each notebook. What's in the Trash still counts until you delete it for good.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. The Plus screen shows the exact day you'll be charged, and the app can remind you 2 days before the trial ends, if you want. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in Estante Settings, under the gear at the top of the bookcase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, tap the gear at the top of the bookcase, then Manage Subscription. Or open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Estante.

What happens if I stop subscribing?

Nothing gets deleted. You can still write in your 3 oldest notebooks, the ones you created first. The others stay put away on your bookcase, just as they are, and open again with Plus. And you can export any notebook whenever you want.

I switched iPhones and Plus didn't show up.

Tap +, then Estante Plus, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you made the purchase with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, and Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.
